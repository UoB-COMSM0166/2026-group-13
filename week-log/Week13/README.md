# Week 13 Tasks: Final Development, Final Version, and Video Preparation

<br>

In Week 13, our team continued developing *U help U* and completed the **final version** of the game. This week focused on finalising gameplay features, polishing the player experience, preparing the final report, and recording materials for the final demonstration video.

## Final Version Completed

By the end of Week 13, we completed the final version of *U help U*. This version integrates the main gameplay, UI, level design, and supporting systems into a more coherent player experience.

## Final Evaluation and Testing

In previous weeks, we conducted both qualitative and quantitative evaluation work. By Week 13, we still find new testers to try our game and the focus shifted to final checks rather than large new evaluation rounds.

The final checks focused on:

- Whether players can understand the main controls.
- Whether tutorial guidance is clear enough.
- Whether the record-and-replay mechanic works reliably.
- Whether the game can be demonstrated smoothly.
- Whether the final version avoids major blocking bugs.
- Whether the UI and language settings work during presentation.
- Whether key game flows can be shown in the final video.

These checks helped us confirm that the final build was suitable for submission, demonstration, and report writing.

## Demonstration Preparation

We also practised demonstrating the game so that the final presentation and video could focus on the most important features.

The demonstration plan focuses on the following order:

1. Briefly introduce the game concept.
2. Show the main menu and level entry.
3. Demonstrate basic movement.
4. Demonstrate recording and replaying.
5. Show how the clone helps solve puzzles.
6. Show our two technical challenges: leaderboard with account system and map editor.
7. Demonstrate our hard, easy, special levels.
